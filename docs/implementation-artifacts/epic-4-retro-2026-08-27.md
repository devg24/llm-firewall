## ⚠️ Challenges & Discoveries
1. **Directory Traversal Performance & Symlink Cycles:**
   - Unbounded filesystem walks in large projects or node_modules could cause startup latency or stack overflow.
   - *Resolution:* Enforced aggressive ignore list (`target`, `node_modules`, `.git`, `.venv`), canonical path deduplication, and a strict depth limit of 5.
2. **ReDoS Risk in User-Defined Regex Rules:**
   - User-supplied regex rules in `.guardian.toml` could introduce catastrophic backtracking.
   - *Resolution:* Enforced linear-time DFA compilation via `regex::RegexBuilder` with bounded size limits and diagnostic rejection on invalid patterns.
